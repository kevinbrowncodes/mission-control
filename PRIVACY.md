# Privacy Policy — Mission Control

Mission Control (the macOS menu bar app by Kevin Brown) does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data.

- The app runs entirely on your Mac. It has no network access and contacts no server.
- It reads the list of running applications and, once you grant Accessibility access, the titles of their windows, only to show them in its panel. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
- The only setting it stores is its own preferences (Open at Login, the global shortcut toggle), in the app's sandbox container.
- There are no accounts, analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/kevinbrowncodes/mission-control.
